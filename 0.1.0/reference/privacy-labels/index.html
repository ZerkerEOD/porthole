    Store privacy answers¶

    Maintainer answer sheet for App Store Connect (App Privacy) and Google Play Console (Data safety), kept in step with https://zerkersec.io/privacy. Update both forms whenever section 6 or 7 of the policy changes. Last reviewed 2026-11-12.

    Shared facts¶

    • Privacy policy URL: https://zerkersec.io/privacy. Terms: https://zerkersec.io/terms.
    • In-app link: Settings → "Privacy policy" and "Terms of service" (app/lib/screens/settings.dart).
    • No accounts in the app. No in-app purchases. No ads. No analytics or crash-reporting SDK. The app has a built-in demo machine ("Try the demo" on the first screen) that reviewers can use.
    • Third-party SDKs that touch the network: Firebase Core + Firebase Messaging (push token only; analytics deactivated in AndroidManifest.xml and Info.plist). mobile_scanner scans QR codes on device; on Android it uses Google ML Kit's barcode model, which processes frames locally.
    • Permissions: INTERNET, CAMERA (QR pairing), POST_NOTIFICATIONS, FOREGROUND_SERVICE and FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC (keeps the VPN socket alive while a session is open). iOS: NSCameraUsageDescription.
    • Data the app sends to the developer's servers: only when the user pastes a relay URL for the developer-hosted relay and turns push on. Then the relay receives the credential from that URL, a random device id, platform and the push token, and the daemon later sends encrypted notices the relay cannot read. The relay operator (the developer, for the hosted relay) links the device id to the website account the credential belongs to. Users who paste their own relay's URL send nothing to the developer. Everything else stays between the phone and the user's own computer over their VPN.
    • The developer account is an individual. Apple shows the legal name as the seller; Google shows the legal name, country and developer email. No address is shown because the app is free with no in-app purchases, so there is no "monetised" trigger.

    Apple: App Privacy (App Store Connect)¶

    Question Answer
    Do you or your third-party partners collect data from this app? Yes
    Contact Info Not collected (the supporter email is handled on the website, not in the app)
    Identifiers → Device ID Collected. Linked to the user: Yes (the hosted relay records the device id against the website account whose credential was used). Used for tracking: No. Purpose: App Functionality. (The random push device id and the push token, sent to the relay only when push is on.)
    User Content, Usage Data, Diagnostics, Location, Financial, Health, Browsing, Search, Purchases, Contacts, Sensitive Info Not collected
    Tracking No. NSPrivacyTracking is false in app/ios/Runner/PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy.
    Privacy manifest app/ios/Runner/PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy: DeviceID collected, linked, App Functionality; UserDefaults accessed for reason CA92.1.

    Note: the camera is used for QR scanning only; images are not collected, so no Photos or Videos data type applies.

    Google: Data safety (Play Console)¶

    Section Answer
    Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types? Yes
    Is all user data encrypted in transit? Yes (TLS to the daemon with certificate pinning; HTTPS to the relay)
    Do you provide a way for users to request data deletion? Yes: remove the relay in Settings, unpair the phone on the daemon, delete the account at zerkersec.io/supporter (hosted relay users), or email privacy@zerkersec.io
    Device or other IDs Collected: yes. Shared: yes, with Google (FCM push token) as a service provider. Ephemeral: no. Required: optional (only when push is enabled). Purpose: App functionality.
    App activity, App info and performance (crash logs, diagnostics), Personal info, Financial, Location, Messages, Photos and videos, Audio, Files, Calendar, Contacts, Health Not collected
    Does the app use any security practices? Data encrypted in transit; users can request deletion; follows the Families policy: not applicable (not directed at children)
    Independent security review No

    Camera: used for QR codes only, frames are processed on device and discarded; nothing to declare under Photos and videos.

    Other console answers¶

    • Encryption / export compliance (Apple): the app uses only standard, published algorithms (TLS, X25519, HKDF-SHA256, XChaCha20-Poly1305) for its own protocol, so ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption is false in Info.plist. Aaron confirms the exemption reading and files the annual self-classification report with BIS if it applies to him.
    • Foreground service (Play, dataSync): "Keeps the connection to the user's own computer open while they watch a session, so prompts arrive without a cloud service. Started only from the Settings switch, stopped from the same switch or by closing the app." Attach the demo recording.
    • IP addresses: Cloudflare's short-lived request logs apply to the relay (policy section 3); not a collected data type in either form.
